Glossary

CY — Compressed Yeast
A fresh form of yeast made of Saccharomyces cerevisiae fungi (from ceravisia, Latin for beer).
IDY — Instant Dry Yeast
A dried form of Saccharomyces cerevisiae yeast.
ADY — Active Dry Yeast
A dried form of Saccharomyces cerevisiae yeast that requires activation in warm water.
SSD — Stiff Sourdough
A form of sourdough at 50% hydration.
LSD — Liquid Sourdough
A form of sourdough at 100% hydration.
OD — Old Dough
A portion of dough kept from day(s) before and added to the new dough.
Poolish
Liquid pre-ferment with 1:1 flour:water and little yeast, left to ferment at 18-20°C. Increases dough extensibility. Over 12 h requires flour with W > 300.
Biga
Solid pre-ferment, flour:water 1:0.45 with 1% fresh yeast, fermented 24 h at 18°C (or 48 h at 13-14°C). Gives fragrance, crunchiness, preservability and digestibility. Requires flour with W > 350.
Autolysis
Yeast-free pre-mix of flour and water, used to increase water absorption and reduce processing times. Over 5-6 h it is advisable to add part of the dough salt.
RT
Room Temperature. With CT on, the RT fields describe the room-temperature stage before the fridge (punta): the yeast math integrates RT + CT into one continuous rise. The post-fridge warm-up stage is not modelled — for pan pizza take the dough out 3-4 h before baking.
CT
Controlled Temperature (e.g. fridge).
W — Flour strength
Medium-strong flour W ~ 260 is ideal for Neapolitan pizza; strong flour W ~ 350 is ideal for Roman pan pizza.
Neapolitan
Medium-hydrated dough (~60%), single-stage rise at RT, hand-formed: 4 mm center, 1-2 cm border; baked in a wood-fired stone oven at 485°C for 60-90 s.
Pan Pizza (Roman)
Highly hydrated dough (~80%) with three fermentation stages: 1-2 h RT, 24-72 h CT (4°C), then 3-4 h RT; baked in sheet pans at 300°C for 10-15 min.

About

Single-file re-implementation of PizzApp (fisico.pizzapp) — the ingredient and yeast calculation of the app, reverse-engineered from its obfuscated Cordova JavaScript. All computation runs locally in your browser; saved recipes live in this browser's localStorage.

The indications derive from experience and are an aid, not a replacement for practice.

Calculation options

Features enabled on the dough screen

Enable only what you use — the dough screen shows just the matching fields (like the original app's settings page).

Poolish and Biga are both enabled: flour, water, yeast and the main-dough doses subtract BOTH pre-ferments — this app intentionally improves on the original, whose Biga block overwrote the Poolish doses.
Sourdough + pre-ferment: the yeast amounts are grams added across two different methods — treat the total as indicative.
